My doctoral research connected device physics with system-level validation. I modeled lasers, micro-LEDs, photodetectors, and photonic components using tools including COMSOL, Lumerical, and Python. I then fabricated and characterized devices to evaluate how their optical and electrical behavior affected performance in communication, imaging, and hardware security.
